# Code of Conduct

sk is built in the open, and the people around it come from very different backgrounds.
We want everyone to be able to take part without being harassed, belittled or excluded.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and direct. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, ask before you judge, and accept that others may know things you do not.
- Give credit, take responsibility for mistakes, and help others learn from theirs.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the open where the project can learn from them.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, demeaning comments or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination based on who someone is, how they look, where they come from or what they believe.
- Publishing other people's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or issues.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project happens: the repository, issues and pull requests, releases, and any
place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code, contact the maintainer by email
at the address in the repository's commit history. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what action is appropriate: a private note, a public warning, removal
of content, or exclusion from the project. They explain their decision to the people involved.
